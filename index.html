About Us

Sydney's Trusted
Approvals Partner

Based in Quakers Hill, Project Approvals is a specialist consultancy guiding residential and commercial projects through NSW's complex planning and approval processes. We combine deep technical expertise with a practical, client-first approach.

  • ✦Deep knowledge of Western Sydney council requirements and LEPs
  • ✦Strong relationships with council assessment officers
  • ✦Fast turnaround times without sacrificing quality or compliance
  • ✦Transparent fixed-fee pricing — no surprise invoices, ever
  • ✦Full documentation: pre-DA through to occupation certificate
  • ✦Residential, dual-occ, multi-dwelling and commercial projects
